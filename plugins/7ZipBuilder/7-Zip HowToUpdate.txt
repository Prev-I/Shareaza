1. Download the latest "LZMA SDK" (lzmaXXXX.7z) from https://www.7-zip.org/sdk.html (the files are on github.com/ip7z/7zip releases; check the asset digest GitHub shows)
2. Copy IArchive.h and every header it includes, directly or not, from "C\", "CPP\Common\" and "CPP\7zip\" to "7zxr\C\", "7zxr\CPP\Common\" and "7zxr\CPP\7zip\"
3. Download the matching "7-Zip Extra" (7zXXXX-extra.7z) from https://www.7-zip.org/download.html
4. Copy "7zxa.dll" to "7zxa.dll", "x64\7zxa.dll" to "7zxa64.dll", and "License.txt" to "7zxa license.txt"
